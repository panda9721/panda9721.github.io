Breakout Blueprint

A cooperative/solo tabletop card game where players work to execute an escape plan from prison before alertness peaks or resources run dry.

01 — OVERVIEW

Tactical Prison Escape &
Card Mechanics.

Breakout Blueprint is a strategic tabletop card game developed as part of a school team project. Players take on the role of inmates attempting to complete 3 Plan Cards to escape prison before reaching 10 Alertness or depleting the draw deck.

Working in a team of 4, I was responsible for the **card layout design**, **cardback artwork**, **DLC expansion card designs**, and designing the mechanics for the **10 core Ability Cards**.

03 — GAME SYSTEM & RULES

Rule Engine &
Win/Loss Conditions.

Card Types (40 Total)

• 20 Tool Cards: Deal damage to enemies or complete plans.
• 10 Ability Cards: Temporary effects and utility.
• 5 Plan Cards: Escape requirements.
• 5 Enemy Cards: Obstacles raising alertness.

Turn Flow

1. Draw Phase: Draw 4 cards, keep 3, shuffle 1 back.
2. Play Phase: Take up to 3 actions (play cards/tools).
3. Resolution Phase: Check defeated enemies & completed plans.
4. Enemy Phase: Active enemies attack & trigger effects.

Victory Condition

Successfully attach the required Tool Cards to complete 3 Plan Cards to escape the prison.

Loss Conditions

• Reach 10 Alertness Tokens.
• Run out of cards in the Draw Deck during Resolution.
