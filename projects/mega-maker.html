GAME DESIGN • LEVEL DESIGN • DIGIPEN BILBAO

Mega Maker

A level design project focused on creating original, playable levels with intentional gameplay cadence, thematic identity, and escalating difficulty.

01 — OVERVIEW

Designing levels
from the ground up.

Mega Maker is a level editor that I used to design and build original playable levels from scratch.

This was an individual project completed as part of my design module during my exchange at DigiPen Bilbao in Spain.

I was responsible for the entire design process, including level layouts, gameplay flow, thematic direction, difficulty progression, and the placement of gameplay elements.

Each level was designed with gameplay cadence in mind, balancing moments of challenge, movement, exploration, and recovery.

02 — DESIGN

Built around
gameplay rhythm.

The levels were designed around intentional gameplay cadences, using changes in challenge and pacing to create a structured player experience. I also developed distinct themes and difficulty levels for each stage.

05 — TAKEAWAYS

What I learned.

This project gave me hands-on experience with designing gameplay experiences from the perspective of a level designer.

I learned how important pacing and gameplay cadence are when structuring a level, and how difficulty can be communicated through level design rather than simply making individual obstacles harder.

More importantly, designing the levels entirely myself allowed me to explore how creative decisions and technical constraints work together to create an enjoyable player experience.
